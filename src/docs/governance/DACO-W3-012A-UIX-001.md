## MISSION

Modernize the entire NUPS / GlyphLock front-end into a world-class venue operating system that is intuitive, fast, visually organized, and optimized for real-world use in clubs, hospitality, entertainment, and multi-location businesses.

The application must evolve from an admin-style interface into a polished operational platform where every user immediately understands what to do based on their role.

This directive is a **non-destructive modernization**. Existing functionality is to be preserved, enhanced, reorganized, and unified.

---

## NON-DESTRUCTIVE MODERNIZATION POLICY

- **Preserve** — No existing business logic may be removed.
- **Consolidate** — Merge redundant workflows into authoritative interfaces.
- **Enhance** — Improve visual clarity and interaction design.
- **Reorganize** — Relocate controls to intentional, role-appropriate locations.
- **Modernize** — Update visual language and interaction patterns.
- **Clarify** — Improve labels, grouping, and progressive disclosure.

No workflow may disappear. No functionality may become inaccessible.
