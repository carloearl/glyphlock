# GlyphLock Public Claims Policy

This document defines how public statements about GlyphLock should be written and verified.

## Evidence classes

### Implemented
A feature exists in the current codebase and can be demonstrated in the application.

### Operational / deployed
A feature is being used in a real operating environment. Deployment claims should identify the evidence owner and date internally before publication.

### Integration work
GlyphLock has built or tested interoperability with a third-party technology. This does not automatically mean the third party is a partner, sponsor, certifier, or endorser.

### Internal standard or research
A GlyphLock-authored framework, simulation, methodology, covenant, protocol, or internal standard. These must not be described as externally certified, judicially validated, regulator-approved, or industry-standard unless independent evidence exists.

### Third-party verified
A certification, registration, patent, court ruling, partnership, audit, or regulatory status backed by an identifiable independent source.

## Required wording rules

- Do not use `certified`, `registered`, `patented`, `judicially validated`, `court upheld`, `partner`, or similar third-party-validation language without documentary support.
- Use `aligned with` only when the technical or operational basis is documented, and do not present alignment as certification.
- Use `integration`, `interoperability`, `tested with`, or `built for` when that is the actual relationship.
- Distinguish revenue, bookings, pipeline, escrow, stored value, and projections; do not present one as another.
- Treat simulations and internal research as simulations and internal research.
- Correct inaccurate public claims promptly and preserve the corrected record.

## Canonical sources

The active application code and the current `main` branch of `carloearl/glyphlock` are the primary technical sources. Older repositories and archived pages may be stale.
